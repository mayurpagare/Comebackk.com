FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers to Your Exam Anxieties

Everything you need to know before putting your study plan on autopilot.

Can I really pass an engineering exam by studying only 3 chapters?

Yes! University question papers typically have internal choice (e.g. "Attempt 4 questions out of 6"). By thoroughly preparing the top 3 highest-weightage modules, you can answer enough questions to reach 32-40 attempted marks—comfortably clearing the 28/70 passing cutoff without spreading yourself thin.

What is the difference between Detention Break mode and Ongoing Backlog mode?

If you are on a detention break, you don't have immediate exam timetables, so our engine creates a steady, week-by-week foundational mastery plan. If you are attending regular classes with an upcoming exam date, it calculates a daily high-intensity sprint timeline based on days remaining.

How are the repeated PYQ questions curated?

We analyze the last 5 years of university question papers for common question themes, compulsory numerical types, and recurring theoretical definitions to identify the top 10 highest-probability questions per chapter.

Is Comeback.com free to use?

Yes, 100% free. It was built by engineering seniors who experienced backlogs firsthand and wanted to build the tool they wished they had during their toughest semesters.
